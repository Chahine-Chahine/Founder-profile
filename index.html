Health · AI system

Dietitians AI System

The problem: dietitians spend hours per client writing meal plans, answering the same questions and chasing check-ins — time they can't bill.

  • AI drafts personalised meal plans from the client's intake (goals, allergies, preferences); the dietitian reviews and approves
  • Client intake forms feed one profile instead of scattered notes and chats
  • Progress tracking and check-in reminders without manual follow-up
  • An assistant answers routine client questions between sessions

Business impact: more clients per dietitian, with the expert still in control of every plan.

Trade · B2B e-commerce

Sherri Trading Company

The problem: a traditional trading company selling through phone calls and showroom visits, with no online channel and no clear view of what sells.

  • Alibaba-style storefront — wholesale catalog with tiered pricing and minimum order quantities
  • E-commerce checkout for direct online orders
  • Retailer dashboard — retailers reorder, track orders and see their invoices on their own
  • Sales dashboard — revenue, top products and regions in one view
  • Super admin — manage products, retailers, roles and approvals across the platform

Business impact: a 24/7 sales channel, and decisions based on data instead of guesswork. Built on real marketplace catalog data from ohbazzar.com.

FAQ

Questions companies ask me at the booth.

Can you work with our existing tools and data?

Yes. Most projects connect to what you already use — spreadsheets, your current website, WhatsApp, ERP or POS exports. We only replace what's slowing you down.

How do you price projects?

Fixed scope and fixed price for defined builds, or a monthly retainer for ongoing work. You get the number in writing before anything starts.

Do you sign NDAs and white-label agreements?

Yes. For white-label work your client never sees our name, and the code and IP belong to you or your client.

How fast can you start?

Discovery can happen this week. Build timelines depend on scope; small websites and chatbots take weeks, not months.

Do you work with companies outside Lebanon?

Yes. We work remotely, with clear weekly updates and async communication across time zones.
